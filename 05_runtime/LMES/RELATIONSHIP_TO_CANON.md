# LMES Relationship to Canonical Lattice (v1.2)

**Status:** Relationship declaration  
**Purpose:** Make explicit the parentage and scope limits of LMES relative to the rest of the vault.  
**Rule:** LMES does not replace, supersede, or silently inherit the full constitutional / structural invariant planes.

---

## 1. Positioning

LMES is a **minimal operational projection** of selected Lattice principles.
It is intentionally smaller than the full architecture.

It does **not** claim to implement:
- the complete constitutional invariant set (S1 / S2)
- the full structural Tier-1 set (S3)
- subsystem- or system-level halt semantics
- Stumpy evidence acquisition
- Vault Chain persistence

---

| LMES Invariant | Closest Canonical Parent(s) | Relationship Type | Notes / Residual Tension |
|----------------|-----------------------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| Operator Primacy | S1-III-07 (Governance supersedes runtime); S2-08 (Operator identity is stable) | PROJECTS / REFINES | Strong alignment. LMES operationalizes checkpoint control. |

| Non-Obfuscation | Score Honesty / Non-fabrication principles; S3-IV-SIL (Silence Is Structural) | PROJECTS / REFINES | Partial. LMES focuses on surfacing assumptions rather than silence-as-state. |

All other constitutional and structural invariants are **explicitly out of LMES scope**.

---

## 3. Explicit Non-Claims

- LMES does not resolve the six / seven / ten invariant ontology problem.
- LMES does not implement Article VIII "halt execution" at subsystem or system scope.
- LMES Audit Logs are text artifacts; they are not automatically written to Vault Chain.
- LMES does not assert authority over Stumpy, Vara, or Veil.

---

## 4. Intended Use

LMES may be used as:
1. A standalone governed reasoning shell in any text environment.
2. A bounded execution surface whose Audit / Failure Logs can later be presented as evidence to Stumpy.
3. A teaching / onboarding surface for operator control patterns.

It must not be treated as a substitute for the full Lattice governance stack.
